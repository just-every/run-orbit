# Orbit rulings

Recorded scope decisions keep their dates and ruler. No per-seed RL-101 or later cleanup rulings were supplied.

### RL-1 — Collaborator entry grants

Date: 2026-10-19. Ruled by: ct. Governs: RECORD-017, RECORD-023.

A collaborator opens a mention only through a live grant on that record that includes its notes, calls and meetings. The collaborator grant carries an Include notes, calls and meetings option, off by default, set by whoever shares; the installation owner acts for the administrator until team roles ship. Creating the grant and each change are audited with who changed it and the old and new value.

### RL-2 — Permanent installation-origin opt-out

Date: 2026-10-20 07:15Z. Ruled by: ct. Governs: OUT-029.

CAN-SPAM requires the opt-out to keep working for at least 30 days after the send; a link that works forever is the honest choice. The independent single-recipient managed-relay handler is approved; the handler does not depend on mailbox sending shipping first.

### RL-3 — Opt-out upgrade continuity

Date: 2026-10-19. Ruled by: ct. Governs: OUT-020, OUT-032.

An installation upgraded with users already present keeps one-to-one opt-out on until an administrator changes it, and the page shows that basis. Messages approved before the setting existed keep their original unsubscribe group.

### RL-4 — Independent dashboard Slack schedules

Date: 2026-10-22 22:40Z. Ruled by: ct. Governs: REPORT-004.

Slack is a separate destination: the installation owner may explicitly enable a saved schedule version for a channel that chose Dashboard ready, regardless of its email recipients. Every new schedule version, restore, reconnect or destination safety suspension leaves Slack paused; it never auto-resumes or replays historical notices.

### RL-5 — Missing means no recorded stakeholder

Date: 2026-10-19 08:38Z. Ruled by: ct. Governs: AGENT-002.

A missing stakeholder means no contact is recorded in that role on the deal. The agent shows recorded roles as observations; any suggested stakeholder is a labelled inference with its source.

### RL-6 — The last-shipping integration carries the complete walk

Date: 2026-10-19 06:03Z. Ruled by: ct. Governs: BOUNDARY-001.

The full cross-product walk is carried by INTEGRATE-001–008, CALL-001/002 and QUOTE-001, each carrying its step in its own text and acceptance. Whichever ships last runs the complete walk.

### RL-7 — Slack identity and audience conditions

Date: 2026-10-20 18:20Z and 19:12Z. Ruled by: ct. Governs: INTEGRATE-005, INTEGRATE-009, INTEGRATE-010.

A Slack identity mapping is explicit and administrator-confirmed, never inferred from email. Channel routing never widens record or field access, including across shared or changed audiences.
