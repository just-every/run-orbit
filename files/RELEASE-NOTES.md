Orbit 0.4.9 adds saved record views, separately authorized Gmail sending and reviewed imports from a connected Pipedrive account. Orbit still runs on rer 0.2.107.

- **Save the record lists you use.** Save a contact, company or deal list with its search, filters, visible columns and sort order. Keep it private or make it available to the workspace, update or delete it, and reopen it against current records and permissions. Saved views retain definitions, not a frozen copy of the results. Archived custom fields remain readable in saved views, and deal amounts use the workspace currency.
- **Choose an authorized Gmail mailbox when reviewing a message.** Reconnect a Google capture source with separate mailbox-send consent, then select that mailbox in the draft's send review. Approval binds the saved recipient, rendered content and sending mailbox; a changed review needs fresh approval. Sent history identifies the mailbox. A paused, disconnected or refused mailbox does not switch to another sender. Uncertain delivery is reconciled against Sent without automatically submitting the same approval again. Microsoft mailbox sending remains unavailable because its connection does not request the permission needed to create a draft. Read access and calendar writeback stay separate from send consent.
- **Review a connected Pipedrive import before applying it.** The installation owner can supply a read-only OAuth token and pull organizations, people, USD deals, pipeline and stage metadata, supported scalar custom fields and linked notes into the existing saved dry-run and Apply flow. Pulls keep checkpoints across interruptions and rate limits. Map source stages and contact or company owners before previewing; deal owners remain source history. New contacts start as Do not contact, missing source marketing status also holds an existing match, and existing stops win. This pull requires a USD workspace, never converts amounts, and is limited to 1,000 rows per entity and 2 MB of source review data. Leads, products, activities, files and automations need separate reconciliation; unsupported fields retain source references and export guidance. Orbit writes nothing back to Pipedrive. Credentials, checkpoints and connected review authority stay out of archives; reconnect and pull again after a restore.
- **Draft and activity wording follows what happened.** A reply that makes a handwritten draft stale asks for review without claiming a scheduled follow-up was cancelled. Contact permission uses ordinary wording in that review. Record activity names task creation, completion, cancellation, reassignment, rescheduling and recurring occurrences.
- **Record and workflow pages recover more consistently.** A missing record offers the same recovery when related reads finish in a different order. Previewing an active workflow's draft keeps one editor. Older native archives remain recognized, with new mailbox-history fields initialized from their migration defaults and restored mailbox send authority withheld until fresh consent.

Account proposals remain limited to selected written evidence and the existing three proposal types. Scheduled graph maintenance, transcripts, licensed enrichment and broader stakeholder inference remain incomplete. Workflow undo remains partial. The earlier erasure limitations remain: a former sponsor or approver can still appear by name in a colleague's timeline and audit rows, and an approver's name stays in the search index until it is rebuilt. These changes do not establish complete agent coverage, complete migration from another CRM or live-provider acceptance.
