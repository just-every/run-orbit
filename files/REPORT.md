## Cause and repair

Previously contactBasis inferred the current inquiry wording from a form without a marketing checkbox. Existing publications continued to serve their old approved artifact, so a submission could falsely record wording never shown.

Capture now loads and verifies the operation's actual artifact. Its bytes/hash are fenced in the same atomic capture batch. An inquiry basis exists only if that reviewed fixed artifact carries the disclosure element with the exact disclosed wording. Otherwise the erasable signal retains contactBasis=null and authenticated submissions return contact_basis=null. A new contact without an accepted basis remains do_not_contact. Existing contact state and prior stops remain governed by the existing intake rules. The submissions panel explicitly shows “No contact basis recorded.” Contemporary marketing consent, fresh disclosed inquiry forms, replay and erasure retain their existing paths.

No migration, historical submission rewrite or artifact rewrite was added. No assertion was deleted, skipped or weakened in the repository. The suite budget remains 198 cases + 2 qualifications = 200.
